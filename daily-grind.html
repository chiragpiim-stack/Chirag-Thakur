01 — Social media · Campaign concept

The Daily Grind

A 30-day social campaign concept for a neighborhood coffee brand, built to make everyday moments recognizable and give the community a reason to return.

01 / The brief

The Challenge

Help a neighborhood coffee shop stand out in a crowded local feed without relying on constant discounts or disconnected one-off posts. The campaign needed a repeatable voice and a simple path from discovery to a visit.

02 / Who it is for

Strategy & Audience

Focus on nearby coffee drinkers looking for a reliable daily ritual: commuters, remote workers, and people meeting friends. Position the shop as a warm, familiar neighborhood stop, with content that balances product appeal and human moments.

03 / What gets made

Execution Steps

  • Build a 30-day calendar around product, people, place, and community moments.
  • Develop a consistent visual direction and reusable post/story templates.
  • Mix short-form video, photo stories, community prompts, and clear visit-oriented calls to action.
  • Review performance weekly and adjust creative based on audience response.

04 / How to evaluate it

Key Takeaways/Metrics

Keep the cadence sustainable and make each post serve a clear role. Track local reach, saves, shares, profile actions, direction requests, and campaign-attributed visits where measurement is available. These are proposed KPIs; no live campaign results are claimed.
