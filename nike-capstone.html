03 — Performance marketing · Capstone

Nike Capstone Strategy

A multi-channel performance strategy connecting campaign objectives to channel roles, a practical budget allocation model, and a measurement framework.

01 / The brief

The Challenge

Translate a broad brand and growth ambition into an actionable digital plan. The capstone needed to show how channel selection, investment, and measurement could work together rather than treating each platform as an isolated tactic.

02 / Who it is for

Strategy & Audience

Organize the plan around customer intent and journey stage: discovery audiences, active product researchers, and high-intent shoppers. Match each segment with suitable creative and channel roles while preserving a consistent brand story.

03 / What gets made

Execution Steps

  • Set campaign objectives and map each to a funnel stage and audience signal.
  • Assign paid search, paid social, and supporting content distinct jobs.
  • Build a budget allocation model that can be reviewed against performance and learning goals.
  • Define tracking conventions, reporting views, and a regular optimization cycle.

04 / How to evaluate it

Key Takeaways/Metrics

Give every channel a measurable role, then use consistent attribution and review cadence to guide investment. Monitor reach and frequency, click-through rate, conversion rate, cost per acquisition, and return on ad spend. These are proposed KPIs; no live campaign results are claimed.

Academic capstone concept. Strategy and measurement plan are illustrative; results are not represented as live-client outcomes.
