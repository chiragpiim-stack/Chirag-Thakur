02 — Brand strategy · Funnel concept

Flavour Fusion

An audience-led positioning and funnel concept for a contemporary food brand, connecting customer needs to relevant messages at each stage.

01 / The brief

The Challenge

Create a clear route to market for a contemporary food concept in a busy category. The brand needed a reason to be chosen beyond broad claims about taste and quality, plus a customer journey that connected discovery to trial.

02 / Who it is for

Strategy & Audience

Use segmentation to distinguish customers by occasions and motivations, then prioritize the groups whose needs align with the concept. Define positioning around a distinct food experience and tailor messages to each selected audience rather than treating everyone as one segment.

03 / What gets made

Execution Steps

  • Map the category, customer occasions, and competing alternatives.
  • Build an STP framework: segment the market, prioritize target groups, and articulate a differentiated position.
  • Design a funnel from awareness content to consideration proof points and a low-friction first order.
  • Align channel, creative, and landing-page messages; test variations before scaling.

04 / How to evaluate it

Key Takeaways/Metrics

Make the value proposition specific to a real audience need and carry it consistently through the funnel. Monitor qualified reach, engagement, landing-page conversion, first-order conversion, and repeat purchase. These are proposed KPIs; no live campaign results are claimed.
